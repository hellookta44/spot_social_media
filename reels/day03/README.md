## Storyboard

| Time | Footage | On-screen text | Sound |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–3s | Pink pipette macro, slow motion | **The final cut.** — Round two begins. | Pad + bell shimmer |
| 3–8s | Unboxing: flaps, bubble wrap, bottle reveal | **The shortlist just arrived.** — Sealed. Wrapped. Ready for testing. | Cardboard thump, bubble-wrap crinkle and pops, glass clink |
| 8–11s | Bottle lineup (labels blurred, tracked) | **Every formula gets tested again.** | Two glass clinks |
| 11–14s | Founder dropping serum onto the hand | **On our own skin,** *first.* | Drop "plips" |
| 14–18s | Close-up: rubbing it in | **Texture / Absorption / Feel** (word by word) | Soft rubbing swish, ticks |
| 18–21s | Pipette macro, vertical | **Only the best make the final cut.** — Which one makes it? Find out tomorrow. | Drums drop out, swell |
| 21–25s | SPOT. end card | *Day 4: on the road to the factory.* SKINCARE PRODUCTS · COMING SOON / @shopspotbeauty | Resolve + bell |

**Kept out of frame / blurred:** "Vitamin C serum" and other bottle labels, the
manufacturer's name on the box, the shipping label, and the "…mide 10% zinc"
label (the daylight dropper clip is not used for that reason).
